Version 1.1 replacement/restoration provenance

Native normal images were decoded from DXT5nm alpha/green channels already present
in the recovered Kino exports. Manifest mappings and hashes distinguish exact
filename matches from conservative replacement material tuning. New contact AO,
dust, projector light, trap visuals and pooled impact particles are original
procedural replacement work, not original T5 lightmaps or effects.

The 72 additional native movement, impact, Monkey Bomb and Claymore clips were
recovered from the included shared T5 exports, primarily common_zombie.ff. Their
source hashes/paths remain in audio/manifest.json. Original synthesized electric
and fire trap loops are explicitly marked replacements. AUDIO-EVENT-CATALOG.json
records timing, routing, concurrency, provenance and exact missing recordings.

Version 1.2 weapon presentation

Native viewmodel clips, offsets, tags and timing from the recovered exports
support aiming-down, sprint-out, dual reload/last-shot and reload-prop corrections.
Runtime interpretation and transitions are reconstruction code. Existing native
clips do not establish exact T5 animation semantics for every bone or weapon.
The later attachment correction restores native model bind offsets to weapon-part
translation tracks; arm translations and rotations remain unchanged. A narrow
AUG Masterkey pump correction reconciles an inconsistent clip rotation basis
with its native model bind and sprint/raise basis, preserving the pump stroke
and every other bone. See the separate validation report for acceptance evidence.

Version 1.4 detail and combat pass
The user supplied a lobby image as visual reference. No reference photograph or
video was imported as a texture. Native geometry, chandelier, paper props, source
textures and period posters were retained. Soft alpha on original authored decals
was restored. Ten surface-clipped blood, scorch and debris overlay placements are
new reconstructions using native textures, not recovered original layer UVs.
See DRESSING.md for exact provenance and missing original GfxWorld layer buffers.

Six direct-sun floor tiles use the native shell and 3,326 static instances. They
replace only the coarse solar component on matching flat floors, preserving
powered lamps and other diffuse lighting. The original T5 lightmaps remain absent.
Enemy voice occlusion, quiet nearby footsteps, pose retiming, movement-based
weapon motion and directional impact particles are runtime adaptations. Existing
CC-BY-SA creature recordings and native sound asset notices continue to apply.

Version1.4 follow-up material audit restores ten standalone native white-neutral
stain maps as multiplicative decals and one additional map with soft alpha.
This is a reconstruction of omitted blend behavior from source texture evidence;
no native texture pixels, positions, indices or UVs were changed.
